# Check-in FAQ — Customer Support

**Britannic Airways · Help centre**

**When can I check in online?**
From 24 hours before your flight leaves, until 1 hour before it leaves.

**What do I need to find my booking?**
Your 6-character booking reference and the last name on the booking.

**Do I have to pay to choose a seat?**
No. Once check-in is open, choosing a seat is free.

**How long does my passport need to be valid?**
At least 6 months after your trip ends.

**Can I check in my family later if they aren't ready?**
Yes. Check in whoever is ready now. The others can check in later from the same booking.

**When does boarding start?**
45 minutes before departure. Your boarding pass shows the exact time and your gate.
